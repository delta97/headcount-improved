# Accomplishments log

## The failure this prevents

The self-review gets written once a year against a memory that keeps three months. The
accomplishments page exists and is even favorited — but it is fed manually, which means
rarely, which means the review reconstructs the year from weekly pages under deadline. The
wins that survive that process are the recent and the loud, not the largest.

## Sources

Weekly notes pages and structured meeting notes since the last harvest (stamp each harvest's
window at the bottom of the page so the next one knows where to start). Signals of a win:
shipped/launched/approved language, a decision going the owner's way after a contested thread,
a metric moving, a stakeholder's unprompted thanks, a process that stopped being manual.

## The entry format

Impact framing, one entry per accomplishment:

- **What** — one line, outcome-first ("Intake and graduation process approved and adopted",
  not "wrote a process doc").
- **So that** — the business consequence, with the number when one exists (hours saved per
  week, spend protected, revenue enabled, cycle time cut). No number yet is fine — record
  where the number will come from and revisit.
- **Evidence** — links: the shipped thing, the approval note, the meeting where it landed.
  Reviews written from evidenced entries read as fact; unevidenced ones read as claims.
- **Date and workstream** — so review season can slice by quarter and by area.

## Method

1. Sweep the window; draft candidate entries. Over-collect, then cut — a marginal win is
   cheap to delete in April and impossible to recover in review week.
2. Distinguish *done* from *impact landed*. Shipping the pipeline is an entry; "and it has
   run clean for a month, replacing N hours/week" is the better entry a later harvest
   upgrades it to. Leave an "upgrade when" note on entries awaiting their number.
3. Merge into the accomplishments page under its existing structure, newest first within
   workstream. Never rewrite old entries' claims — append upgrades.
4. On request, assemble review material from the page: per-quarter highlights, or the
   strongest evidenced entries mapped against review criteria.

## Rules

- Team wins are logged as team wins with the owner's specific contribution named. Inflated
  attribution is discovered exactly when it costs the most.
- Log losses that taught something separately if at all — this page has one job.
